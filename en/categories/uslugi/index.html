What is in the selection Services

In this collection of 53 offers: 17 with a promotional code and 36 without a separate code. The conditions and deadline are indicated on each card; the final price is confirmed by the store.

How to use promotional codes and promotions

  1. Check if there is a separate promotional code on the card.
  2. For a promotional code, click “Show code and go to store”, copy the code and paste it into the cart.
  3. For a promotion without a code, open the store and check that the desired product or rate is included in the offer.
  4. Check the deadline, minimum order amount, exceptions, delivery and total payment.

The presence of an offer in the feed does not mean manual verification on each product and account.

The source of the conditions is the affiliate feed. The store may change the offer. For purchases made through some links, the project may receive compensation.

The terms and conditions are translated automatically. Check the terms and conditions on the store's website.
