Set or individually: how to compare quantity and unit price

A larger package may cost less per piece and at the same time require more money for the entire order. These two results do not contradict each other. To make a meaningful choice, you need two numbers: how much you will pay now and how much the unit of the product you will actually use costs.

First select the same unit

Compare pieces with pieces, meters with meters, milliliters with milliliters. The name "set" does not indicate the quantity. Check the composition: one set may contain the same products, another - different sizes or additional accessories. Even the same number of items does not make the sets interchangeable.

For consumables, first make sure that the required characteristics and compatibility match. For example, fasteners of a different size may not be considered equivalent replacements simply because they cost less per piece. If part of a mixed set does not suit you, separately count the number of suitable items.

Conditional example of two packages

Let’s say that six identical suitable products cost 540 ₽, and delivery costs 120 ₽. The order will cost 660 ₽, that is, 110 ₽ per product. In the second offer, ten of the same products cost 950 ₽ with free shipping. Unit price - 95 ₽.

The second package is cheaper by 15 ₽ per piece, but requires 290 ₽ more when purchasing: 950 − 660. If you use all ten products, the unit price helps evaluate the volume benefit. If only six are needed and the remaining four are not useful, the actual cost for each item needed will be about 158,33 ₽: 950 ÷ 6. In this scenario, smaller packaging is cheaper. All prices and quantities here are conditional.

Formula and calculation limits

The unit price with delivery is equal to the total cost of the order divided by the number of identical units. If there are several different items in your cart, do not add the same shipping fee to each item as this will inflate the overall result. To choose between baskets, it’s easier to first compare their total cost, and use the calculation per piece as an additional indicator.

  1. Write down the exact model and contents of each package.
  2. Convert the quantity to a common unit: for example, 1,5 liters is 1 500 milliliters.
  3. Take the cost after the available automatic discount and add shipping with mandatory fees.
  4. Divide the total by the quantity, and then separately estimate the number of units that are actually needed.
  5. Check the difference in total amounts: the low price per piece does not negate the additional costs of stock.

How to make a decision

Compare the total cost of two options in order calculator, then check the unit price using the formula above. Do not round intermediate values ​​too early: it is more convenient to round the total to kopecks at the end.

Selecting offers in store catalog, keep the required amount nearby. A large set is useful when all the parts fit and the stock has a clear use. If the choice depends on future need, write down both scenarios - using the entire package and only the part needed now. This way the assumption will remain visible in the calculation.
