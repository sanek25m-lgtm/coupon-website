Data and settings

The theme, language, and favorites are saved in localStorage on your device. They can be removed in your browser settings.

Pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, images can be downloaded from partner servers. These services process network requests according to their own rules. When you go to a store or post a message on GitHub, the rules of the corresponding service apply.

The terms and conditions are translated automatically. Check the terms and conditions on the store's website.
