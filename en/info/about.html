About the project

Couponer is a catalog of promotions via a link for which there is no separate promotional code in the source data. Its task is to help you choose a purchase at the final cost: with delivery, mandatory fees and the same quantity of goods. We separate the advertising percentage from the amount payable and offer a calculator to compare the two options. Code offers are in the Coupon Code project, and here are promotions and price comparison materials.

The terms and conditions were transferred by the partners. We check the data format and timing, but do not pass off the presence of a record as a manual check of the purchase. The project may receive compensation through affiliate links.

The terms and conditions are translated automatically. Check the terms and conditions on the store's website.
