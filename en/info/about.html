About the project

The couponer combines promotional codes and promotions from the partner feed. Code cards allow you to show the code after going to the store; cards without a code are marked as promotions and do not promise to enter a non-existent promotional code. The terms and restrictions are transmitted by the partner, and the final discount must be checked in the cart.

The terms and conditions were transferred by the partners. We check the data format and timing, but do not pass off the presence of a record as a manual check of the purchase. The project may receive compensation through affiliate links.

The terms and conditions are translated automatically. Check the terms and conditions on the store's website.
