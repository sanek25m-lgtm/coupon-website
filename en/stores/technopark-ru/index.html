Checking promotions without code

  1. Open the offer link and search for participating products or rates.
  2. Check to see if authorization, a special payment method, or a specific quantity is required.
  3. Add shipping and mandatory fees to the item price. Consider conditional bonuses for future purchases separately.

If the store still requires a code, but the card does not contain it, the conditions need to be clarified. We do not invent a code and do not promise to activate the discount.
