What to consider in comparison

Use one currency: no conversion here. The price per unit is comparable only for the same product, volume and configuration. Future points and conditional cashback do not reduce the amount of today's payment.

For a set, first divide the price of the set by the number of identical units and enter that number in the quantity field. If the composition of the set is different, such a comparison is not applicable.

An example, not a store suggestion

An item for 1 000 ₽ with delivery 300 ₽ will cost 1 300 ₽. The same product for 1 100 ₽ with free delivery - to 1 100 ₽. In the second case, the payment due is 200 ₽ less.
